# Clarifications

## Round 1

- Q: What time should the dinner start and end?
  A: Start around 20:00
- Q: Is transport needed for guests, including an accessible option for the wheelchair user?
  A: Accessible transport for the wheelchair user only
- Q: Is a rain backup acceptable as an indoor room at the same venue?
  A: Either (indoor room at the same venue or outdoor only is acceptable)
- Q: Should decor, a birthday cake or photography be included?
  A: Cake, decor and photography
