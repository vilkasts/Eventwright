## Recommendation

- Recommended venue: SILK Club
- Venue cost: 3000 EUR

SILK Club is the only shortlisted venue whose page states wheelchair access and elevator access, which covers the [MUST] accessibility requirement (R-08) for the one wheelchair user. It seats up to 60, so 30 guests fit comfortably, and it offers a rooftop terrace plus a panoramic indoor room, private room, sound system and heating with covered areas, which suits live acoustic music and a rooftop dinner (R-04, R-05). It also hosts private celebrations and full buyouts, and offers Mediterranean catering tailored to each event, so vegetarian and gluten-free needs should be arranged with them. The 3000 EUR is an estimate (price on request), about 33% of the 9000 EUR budget and below the 40% cap of 3600 EUR, leaving about 6000 EUR for catering, music, transport, cake, decor and photography; if the minimum spend includes food, catering cost will overlap and should shrink accordingly. The weather verdict is outdoor-ok, and the panoramic indoor room and covered areas also serve as plan B for all guests.
