## Summary

Only the wheelchair user gets arranged transport (an adapted taxi both ways); other guests arrive by taxi, ride-hailing or public transport on their own. The venue states elevator and wheelchair access, and the remaining gaps (step-free street route, accessible toilet) are listed with fixes. Beyond the venue, the plan adds decor, table centerpieces and a 2-hour photographer for an estimated 1120 EUR (photography shortened from 3 to 2 hours to bring the total within the budget limit), all prices being estimates that need quotes.

## Guest transport and parking

- Group transport: none, as requested in clarification round 1 (transport for the wheelchair user only).
- Wheelchair user: pre-booked adapted taxi (boarding platform, wheelchair restraints) from the guest's address to the venue and back. Adapted taxis can be requested from taxi companies in advance, and wheelchairs travel free of charge [1]. Standard Lisbon fare is 0.47 EUR per km with a 3.25 EUR minimum [1]; adapted transfers are quoted individually [2], so a planning allowance of 80 EUR for the round trip is an estimate.
- Pickup plan: outbound pickup at about 19:15 for a 20:00 start; return booked for a fixed time (for example 23:30) with a standby call option.
- Other guests: taxi or ride-hailing (Uber and taxis operate in central Lisbon [1]) and public transport into Chiado; the venue page does not name transport lines, so guests get the address and a map link in the invitation. Exact metro and tram lines are not verified in this run.
- Parking: the venue page mentions nearby parking facilities only, without capacity or cost [3]. Chiado has limited street parking, so guests are advised not to drive; no parking cost is budgeted.
- Drop-off: the taxi should stop as close to the venue entrance as possible; confirm the nearest step-free drop-off point with SILK Club.
