## Summary

In-house catering by SILK Club (the recommended venue) is planned as a seated three-course dinner for 30 guests on 2027-06-12, with a welcome drink, a wine and soft-drink package, and a birthday cake. No menu or event price is published, so the cost is an estimate built on the roughly 50 EUR per person dinner level reported for SILK. After the budget retry the drinks package and cake were trimmed, giving an estimated catering cost of 2790 EUR (195 EUR less than before), about 31% of the 9000 EUR budget. Vegetarian (3) and gluten-free (1) guests are covered by dedicated dishes to be confirmed with the venue.

## Catering option

- Option: in-house catering by SILK Club.
- Source: the venue's events page states SILK offers "Mediterranean dining, cocktail service and tailored catering options designed around each event format" [1]. The page does not say whether external caterers are allowed, so none is planned.
- Format: seated dinner. Reason: the request is a birthday dinner, the venue seats up to 60 for dinner (03-venues.md), a seated format lets the kitchen serve the vegetarian and gluten-free dishes in the same courses as everyone else, and it suits a 20:00 start with live acoustic music.
- Overlap with the venue cost: the 3000 EUR venue figure is a minimum-spend estimate. If the minimum spend includes food and drinks, this catering cost is partly the same money and the total shrinks accordingly; the quote must confirm this.
