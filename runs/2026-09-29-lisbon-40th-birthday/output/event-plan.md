# 40th Birthday Dinner in Lisbon — Event Plan

## Overview

A 40th birthday dinner for 30 guests in Lisbon, Portugal on Saturday 2027-06-12, starting around 20:00. The style is a seated dinner on a rooftop terrace at SILK Club with live acoustic music, vegetarian and gluten-free dishes, wheelchair access, a birthday cake, decor and photography.

The budget total with contingency is 8718.6 EUR against the limit of 9000 EUR, which is 281.4 EUR under the limit. All prices are planning estimates that need written quotes.

A public holiday, National Day, falls on 2027-06-10, two days before the event (no holiday on the event day itself). The venue page does not say whether it opens, closes or charges a surcharge around 10 June, and the long weekend may raise demand for Chiado venues, so request the quote and reserve the buyout early.

## Venue

- Recommended venue: SILK Club (rooftop, Chiado)
- Why: it is the only shortlisted venue whose page states wheelchair access and elevator access. It seats up to 60, offers a rooftop terrace plus a panoramic indoor room, private room, sound system and heating with covered areas, and hosts private celebrations and full buyouts.
- Address and access: Rua da Misericórdia 14, Chiado, near Bairro Alto. Elevator access and wheelchair accessible; nearby parking facilities exist, but the street-to-elevator route, the accessible toilet, public transport lines and noise limits are not stated on the venue page and must be confirmed.
- Opening hours on the event day: 19:00 onwards, Tuesday to Saturday; 2027-06-12 is a Saturday, so the venue is open, and a full buyout is subject to availability.
- Venue cost: 3000 EUR (estimate of a minimum spend, price on request; about 33% of the budget, below the 40% cap of 3600 EUR). If the minimum spend includes food, the catering cost overlaps and should shrink accordingly.
- Alternatives considered: Le Jardin d'Eden (garden restaurant) and Jardim Room at Suspenso (Alfama, private garden); accessibility was not stated on their pages.

## Menu

Format: in-house catering by SILK Club, a seated three-course dinner for 30 guests with a welcome drink, a drinks package (about 3 hours) and a birthday cake. Dishes marked proposed must be confirmed with the events team.

- Welcome: sparkling wine or a non-alcoholic spritz on arrival on the terrace (proposed)
- Starters (shared at the table): tempura shrimps; sushi and sashimi selection; vegetable tempura and vegetarian sushi rolls (proposed, vegetarian)
- Mains (choice of three, pre-ordered with the RSVP): grilled octopus with potatoes; Mediterranean vegetable and cheese main, for example roasted vegetable risotto (proposed, vegetarian); grilled fish or chicken with vegetables and potatoes, prepared without flour or sauces containing gluten (proposed, gluten-free)
- Dessert: birthday cake (about 30 portions, with a gluten-free slice prepared separately, proposed); panna cotta and crème brûlée as plated alternatives (both usually gluten-free, to confirm)
- Drinks package: sparkling welcome drink, house white and red wine, still and sparkling water, soft drinks, coffee and tea for about 3 hours; cocktails from the SILK bar are not included
