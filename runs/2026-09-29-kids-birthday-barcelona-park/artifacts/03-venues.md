## Recommendation
- Recommended venue: Fiesta Privada Barcelona (C/ de Villarroel 99)
- Venue cost: 600 EUR

Fiesta Privada is the only bookable indoor option whose page states a capacity (up to 60), so it establishes room for all 25 guests (15 kids, 10 adults) in a fully covered indoor area, as the indoor-recommended weather verdict requires. The 600 EUR price is 120 EUR per hour with a 5-hour minimum, so it covers the 11:00-14:00 party with setup time and equals the 600 EUR venue share of the 1500 EUR budget, leaving 900 EUR for entertainment, logistics and contingency. Festial is far cheaper at 125 EUR but does not state its capacity or own-food policy, so it is kept only as a backup to confirm by phone. The outdoor Ciutadella lawns cost nothing if the forecast improves, but a tent would not cope with about 11 mm of rain and needs municipal authorization. Own food and drinks are not confirmed on the Fiesta Privada page and should be checked at booking.
