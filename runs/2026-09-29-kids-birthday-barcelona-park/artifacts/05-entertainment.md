## Summary
The party runs 11:00-14:00 at the recommended Fiesta Privada Barcelona (indoor, C/ de Villarroel 99) because the weather verdict is indoor-recommended. Entertainment is one Animaciones Panda animator for 2 hours (110 EUR, travel free within Barcelona) with age-6-to-12 games, within the 150 EUR entertainment share of the 1500 EUR budget. The venue page states that tables, chairs, karaoke, a 70-inch TV and smartphone-controlled sound are included, and that external speakers, DJs and confetti are prohibited, so the program uses only the venue sound system and no confetti. Balloon twisting and face painting are adapted because some kids have non-food allergies (for example latex); the animator must confirm this before booking.

- Kid-friendly items: all animator games are for ages 6-12 and need no equipment that triggers allergies; karaoke is an optional kid-friendly item included in the rental [2].
- Allergy handling (R-09): ask the animator for latex-free options (no balloon twisting, or foil balloons only), hypoallergenic water-based face paint patch-tested on request or skipped, and no nut, feather or strong-fragrance materials. Party favors: unscented stickers, pencils and small notebooks; no candy or scented items.
- Animator working time is 2 hours (11:15-13:15) including the food break, when the animator can pause.
- Venue rules [2]: external speakers, DJs and confetti are not allowed, so the animator must use the venue sound (music is controlled from a smartphone) and no confetti or confetti poppers; the room is soundproofed, so volume is not a constraint.
- Furniture: tables and chairs are included in the rental [2], so no furniture rental is needed for the games or the food break.

## Weather plan B
- No outdoor element depends on weather: the recommended venue Fiesta Privada is fully indoor and the animator program is designed for a room.
- Light fallback: if the forecast improves and the family prefers the Ciutadella lawns, the same games work on open grass; the venue sound system is not available there, so a small speaker would be needed outdoors (the external-speaker ban applies only to the venue [2]). The indoor room stays the rain fallback, and Panda adapts activities to the space [1].
- Confirm with Panda that the booking can move indoors or outdoors up to the morning of 2026-10-07 without penalty (booking is free with no advance payment [1]).
