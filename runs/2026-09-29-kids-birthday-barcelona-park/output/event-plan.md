# Kids' Birthday Party in Barcelona — Event Plan

## Overview

The occasion is the 8th birthday party of a child: a casual party for 15 kids and 10 adults (25 guests) in Barcelona, Spain, on 2026-10-07 (a Wednesday) from 11:00 to 14:00. The family asked for an outdoor park party in Ciutadella and brings its own food and drinks, so catering is not planned.

Because the rain risk is 63%, the recommended venue is the indoor Fiesta Privada Barcelona (C/ de Villarroel 99), with the free Ciutadella lawns as the outdoor option if the forecast improves.

The budget total with contingency is 781.00 EUR against a limit of 1500 EUR. There is no public holiday within 3 days of the event date: 2026-10-09 is a Valencian Community holiday only (regional, does not apply to Barcelona) and 2026-10-12 is the National Day of Spain, 5 days after the event, so no surcharge or earlier booking is expected from holidays. The event is 8 days away, so vendors must be asked for short-notice availability.

## Weather & Plan B

- Method: Open-Meteo forecast for 2026-10-07, Barcelona (event 8 days ahead, within the 14-day forecast limit)
- Key numbers: max 20.9 C, min 16.8 C, rain risk 63%, expected precipitation 11.4 mm, sunset 19:23
- Verdict: indoor-recommended. A tent alone would probably not cope with about 11 mm of rain.

Consolidated plan B:

- The primary plan is already indoor: Fiesta Privada Barcelona is fully covered, holds up to 60 and has heating and cooling, so no outdoor element depends on weather.
- If the forecast improves and the family prefers the Parc de la Ciutadella lawns: free access, an informal picnic needs no permit, but a tent or other temporary structure needs municipal authorization (not budgeted); no open fires or barbecues, clean up all trash.
- Outdoors the venue sound system is not available, so a small speaker would be needed; the external-speaker ban applies only to the venue. The same animator games work on open grass.
- Confirm with Animaciones Panda that the booking can move indoors or outdoors up to the morning of 2026-10-07 without penalty (booking is free with no advance payment).
- Backup indoor room: Festial party room, Carrer de València 137, Eixample, 125 EUR (Wednesday morning slot 10:00-14:00); capacity and own-food policy not stated, confirm by phone.
- Heaters and umbrellas are not needed indoors; furniture backup if the venue lacks chairs is about 2 EUR per chair per day with a minimum of 20 (not budgeted).

## Venue

- Recommended venue: Fiesta Privada Barcelona, C/ de Villarroel 99 (Metro Urgel)
- Venue cost: 600 EUR (120 EUR per hour, minimum 5 hours; book Wednesday 10:00-15:00)
- Why: it is the only bookable indoor option whose page states a capacity (up to 60), so all 25 guests fit in a fully covered room, as the indoor-recommended verdict requires. Tables, chairs, karaoke, a 70-inch TV and smartphone-controlled sound are included; two bathrooms; heating and cooling.
- Access: Metro Urgel, no shuttle needed; guests pay their own fares (single ticket 2.90 EUR, T-casual 13.00 EUR). Parking is not stated on the venue page; advise public transport.
- Rules: external speakers, DJs and confetti are prohibited; noise rules are listed on the venue page, confirm exact limits at booking.
- To confirm at booking: own food and drinks, step-free access, accessible toilet, parking, number of tables and chairs for 25, decor setup before 11:00. No guests have accessibility needs.
