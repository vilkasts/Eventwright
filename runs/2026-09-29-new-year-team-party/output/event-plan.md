# New Year Team Party — Event Plan

## Overview

The occasion is a New Year party for a team, held on 2026-12-31 in Warsaw, Poland, as a casual evening party for 41 guests (the request said "more than 40", so 41 is the planning minimum). The plan uses Pół na Pół at Fort Mokotów with in-house food, plus a photo booth rented from an external vendor. The total with contingency is 5373.5 EUR against the budget limit of 8000 EUR. 2027-01-01 (New Year's Day, national holiday) is the day after the event, so the venue may have different hours or surcharges on that day; the venue page does not state them, and the party itself is not affected.

## Weather & Plan B

- Method: climatology over the last 10 years. The event date is more than 14 days away, so no forecast is available. Historical weather for Dec 28 to Jan 3 over 2016 to 2025 (70 days) was used.
- Key numbers: mean max temperature 4.7 °C, mean min temperature 0.3 °C, rain risk 43% (rain or snow), typical sunset 16:32 local time.
- Verdict: outdoor with a plan B. A fully open-air party is not advisable; the whole evening is in darkness and it is cold.
- Plan B: the heated and air-conditioned indoor studio (190 m², 80 seated, 120 standing) holds all 41 guests under cover and the photo booth is set up in the studio. The terrace and garden are a bonus that is not relied on, because heating of the terrace is not stated on the venue page; if any outdoor area is used, it needs lighting and outdoor heaters.

## Venue

- Recommended venue: Pół na Pół, Fort Mokotów, Szpilmana 6, Warsaw (the second venue of the shortlist, as requested).
- Why: it has a heated indoor studio for all 41 guests plus a terrace and garden (a covered terrace per a third-party listing); capacity is 80 seated and 120 standing; the studio is rented exclusively; loud music, own music, a band, own food and own alcohol are allowed, and late night events are OK.
- Includes: Wi-Fi, professional sound system, professional lighting, microphone, projector and screen, air conditioning, heating and furniture; access to the garden, patio and terrace.
- Access: on-site parking; the venue is about 15 minutes from the Warsaw city centre. Step-free access, accessible toilet, public transport, opening hours on the event day and curfew time are not stated on the venue page; confirm them with the venue.
- Cost: 4599 EUR, an estimate of 20090 PLN for 41 guests at 490 PLN per plate (upper end of a 300 to 490 PLN per-plate range from a third-party listing; the venue publishes no price). The per-plate price includes the menu. This is about 1399 EUR above the venue share of 3200 EUR (40% of 8000 EUR) and overlaps with catering; confirm by quote.
- Photo booth: classic photo booth from an external vendor, VIP package 1250 PLN for 4 hours with operator, unlimited prints, props, backgrounds and online gallery; cost 286 EUR (rate 1 EUR = 4.3685 PLN). No New Year's Eve surcharge is stated on the vendor page.
- Alternatives considered: Pepper Events (24559 PLN, about 5622 EUR) and Gardens by Fort (price on request).

## Menu

- Format: seated dinner at tables in the indoor studio with a relaxed bar for the evening, using the venue's own food (external catering is also permitted).
- Dishes: the venue pages name no dishes, so this is a course structure to confirm with the venue in the quote. Welcome drink and light snacks on arrival; served or shared hot dinner chosen from the venue's menu; sparkling toast and a small sweet table at midnight; bar drinks from the venue (own alcohol allowed without an extra fee).
- Food and drinks per guest: up to 490 PLN (about 112.2 EUR) per plate, already inside the venue cost.
