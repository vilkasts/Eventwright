## Recommendation

- Recommended venue: Pół na Pół (Fort Mokotów, Warsaw), the second venue of the shortlist [2] [3]
- Venue cost: 4599 EUR [5] [4]
- Venue capacity: 80 guests [3]
- Venue includes: Wi-Fi, professional sound system, professional lighting, microphone, projector and screen, air conditioning, heating and furniture; access to the garden, patio and terrace; venue food offered and external catering permitted; the studio is rented exclusively; the 4599 EUR estimate is a per-plate price that also covers the menu, and what exactly it covers beyond that is not stated on the venue page [3] [2] [5]
- Venue rules: loud music OK and own music allowed, a band may be brought, own food and own alcohol allowed without an extra fee, late night events OK; decorations, confetti and curfew time are not stated on the venue page [3]
- Photo booth: classic photo booth rented from an external vendor, Impreza package 1050 PLN for 3 hours or VIP package 1250 PLN for 4 hours with operator, unlimited prints, props, backgrounds and online gallery; VIP is budgeted [6]
- Photo booth cost: 286 EUR [6] [4]

Pół na Pół is the second venue of the shortlist, as the user asked, and it is in Warsaw. The weather plan B needs a covered area for all guests: the heated and air-conditioned indoor studio (190 m2, 80 seated, 120 standing) holds all 41 guests, while the terrace and garden are a bonus that is not relied on because heating of the terrace is not stated on the venue page [3] [2]. Capacity of 80 seated covers 41 guests with room for a casual evening party, and the loud-music, own-food and late-night policies suit a New Year's Eve team party [3]. The price is not published, so 4599 EUR is an estimate at the upper end of the per-plate range for 41 guests; it exceeds the 3200 EUR venue share by about 1399 EUR, but it includes food, so the catering planner should treat it as overlapping with catering, and the venue should be confirmed by quote [5]. The photo booth (286 EUR at 4.3685 PLN per EUR) is a separate rented add-on with no New Year's Eve surcharge stated on the vendor page, and it can be set up in the studio [6] [4].
