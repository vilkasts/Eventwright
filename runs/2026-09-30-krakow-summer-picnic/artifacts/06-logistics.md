## Summary

Guests travel on one chartered 30-seat midi coach from the office on the outskirts of Kraków to Pałac Żeleńskich in Grodkowice (about 25 km) and back. The coach is booked for 8 hours (about 14:00 to 22:00) so it covers the outbound run before 15:00 and the return after 21:00. The published price is from 570 EUR including driver, fuel and tolls [1]. Nothing else needs renting because the venue lists a white tent, a professional sound system and lighting [2]; the tent size must be confirmed for 25 guests. The logistics share is 5% of 3000 EUR = 150 EUR; the cheapest compliant option costs 570 EUR, which exceeds the share by 420 EUR. No cheaper option seats 25 guests: a 23-seat minibus is too small and a 51-seat coach averages about 534 EUR gross [3].
