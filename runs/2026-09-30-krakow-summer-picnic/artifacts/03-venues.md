## Summary

Three Kraków-area outdoor venues with a published capacity were shortlisted for 25 guests on Saturday 2027-07-10 (15:00–21:00, budget 3000 EUR, venue share 1200 EUR). Because the weather verdict is indoor-recommended (61% rain risk), the recommendation is Pałac Żeleńskich in Grodkowice (about 25 km from Kraków), which has a garden plus a white tent. Its published "from 199 PLN per person" price gives about 1140 EUR for 25 guests, inside the 1200 EUR venue share. Caveat: the page does not say whether that per-person price includes food, so the catering planner must not double-count it, and the tent size and venue rules are not stated. No published venue price exists for the other two options, so their prices are labelled estimates.

## Recommendation

- Recommended venue: Pałac Żeleńskich, Grodkowice 1, 32-015 Kłaj [1] [2]
- Venue cost: 1140 EUR (4975 PLN, 25 guests x 199 PLN "from" price per person, converted at 4.3653; the price basis, rental or food and drink spend, is not stated on the venue page) [1] [7]
- Venue capacity: 250 guests seated in the garden [1]
- Venue includes: the page lists food and drinks from the venue, catering, Wi-Fi, professional sound system and lighting, a white tent in the garden and a 15-year event team; which of these the 199 PLN per person covers is not stated on the venue page [1]
- Venue rules: amplified music, decorations, confetti, external food or suppliers and curfew are not stated on the venue page [1]

Pałac Żeleńskich is the only shortlisted venue that combines a green garden with a documented tent [1], which is what the indoor-recommended verdict (61% rain risk) requires: the tent is the covered area for the guests, the band and the food, although its size is not stated on the venue page, so it must be confirmed for all 25 guests. Capacity of 250 seated is far above the 25 guests, so the group can spread out and the band has room. At about 1140 EUR the venue uses 38% of the 3000 EUR budget, inside the 40% share, but it leaves little room if the price turns out to be rental only and food is charged separately. The venue is about 25 km from the city [3], so the shuttle from the office on the outskirts is a longer drive than for a city venue. Wioski Świata is the fallback if the venue price or tent cannot be confirmed: it has a published capacity and parking but no confirmed covered area for all guests and no published price.

## Accessibility and logistics

- Step-free access: not stated on the venue page [1] [2]; no guest has accessibility needs [12].
- Accessible toilet: not stated on the venue page [1] [2].
- Parking: not stated on the venue page [1] [2]; guests arrive by the shuttle, so this is not critical.
- Public transport: not stated on the venue page; the palace is 25 km from Kraków and 12 km from Wieliczka [3], so the shuttle is the practical option.
- Opening hours on the event day (Saturday 2027-07-10): not stated on the venue page [1] [2]; the 15:00–21:00 slot must be confirmed by booking.
- Noise and curfew limits: not stated on the venue page [1]; confirm the band and the 21:00 end when booking.
- Booking terms: preliminary reservations up to 7 days are free and a deposit is due within 3 days of signing the contract [1].
- Public holidays: none within ±3 days [13]
- The holiday check covers national and regional holidays from Nager.Date only, not municipal ones. No holiday falls around 2027-07-10, so no holiday surcharge or earlier booking is expected; the nearest listed holiday is Assumption Day on 2027-08-15.
