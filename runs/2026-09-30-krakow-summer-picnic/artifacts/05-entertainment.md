## Summary

The only entertainment requirement is a live band (R-08) for 25 adult colleagues at Pałac Żeleńskich, Grodkowice, on 2027-07-10 from 15:00 to 21:00. The plan is a cover band playing two sets of about 60 minutes plus light background music between them. No Kraków band publishes a price online, so the band fee is an estimate of 3000 PLN (about 687 EUR), the low end of the Polish live band range [1]. This exceeds the entertainment share (10% of 3000 EUR = 300 EUR) by 387 EUR; no compliant option was found, because the cheapest published band price is already above the share. The venue lists a professional sound system and lighting [3], so no sound or lighting hire is budgeted. No host, photographer or kids' animator is requested, so none is planned.

## Weather plan B

The weather verdict is indoor-recommended (61% rain risk), so the band is planned to be weather-proof from the start [6].

- Band stage in the garden: move under the venue's white tent [3]; the tent size is not stated, so confirm it fits 25 guests plus a 4-piece band, or ask for the palace interior as a second fallback.
- Garden games and free time: move inside the tent or the palace rooms; the games are optional.
- Group photo at 20:15: taken under the tent entrance or in the hall if it rains.
- Decision point: the rain decision is taken 48 hours before the event, and the band is told which stage location applies. Electrical equipment is never placed outside the tent in rain.
