# Kraków company summer picnic — Event Plan

## Overview

The occasion is a company summer picnic in Kraków, Poland, on Saturday 2027-07-10 from 15:00 to 21:00 for 25 guests (company staff, one vegetarian, no accessibility needs). The style is outdoor and casual, held at Pałac Żeleńskich with a white tent as covered fallback, with food, non-alcoholic drinks, a live band and a shuttle from the office on the outskirts of Kraków.
