## Summary
Three châteaux in Île-de-France within about 50 km of Paris were shortlisted for 150 guests on 2027-09-18. Retry fix for G4: Château de Breteuil (1800 EUR) was dropped as the recommendation because its own accessibility page states that the château rooms are hard to reach by wheelchair and says nothing about step-free access to the Orangery [6]. The new recommendation is Château d'Hardricourt, whose listing states disabled access and a published seated capacity of 150 [1]. No compliant venue fits the venue share of 2000 EUR (40% of 5000 EUR): the cheapest compliant one costs a published from-price of 5900 EUR, a gap of 3900 EUR against the share and 900 EUR above the whole 5000 EUR budget, and the caterer is imposed. The budget-aggregator and the coordinator must decide whether to raise the budget or relax the accessibility requirement. The weather verdict is outdoor-with-plan-b, so the indoor orangery and ballroom serve as the covered fallback.

## Recommendation
- Recommended venue: Château d'Hardricourt [1]
- Venue cost: 5900 EUR [1]
- Venue capacity: 150 guests [1]
- Venue includes: exclusive use, non-consumables (linen, lighting), professional sound and lighting system, dedicated staff, on-site accommodation for the couple and guests (7 bedrooms, 14 beds); decor not stated on the venue page [1]
- Venue rules: external caterers not permitted, only approved vendors (Cirette, Lenôtre, Kaspia Réceptions, Réception Prestige); fireworks permitted; amplified music limits, curfew and decoration rules not stated on the venue page [1]

Château d'Hardricourt is a real château 35 km from Paris, so it meets both [MUST]-relevant venue requirements and the search radius, and its listing states disabled access and a seated capacity of exactly 150, matching the guest count [1]. It has French gardens for the ceremony, plus an orangery, a ballroom and several rooms indoors, which covers the garden-plus-indoor requirement. Its from-price of 5900 EUR includes the sound and lighting system and staff, which spares the entertainment and logistics planners some cost. It is the cheapest candidate with stated accessibility, but it is 3900 EUR above the venue share of 2000 EUR and 900 EUR above the whole budget, and the imposed caterer list limits catering savings. Breteuil, at 1800 EUR, fits the budget but is not recommended because step-free access to its dining space is not confirmed [4][5]. The orangery and ballroom are the covered fallback for the garden ceremony (23% rain risk); whether one room holds all 150 seated is not stated on the venue page [1].

## Accessibility and logistics
- Step-free access: disabled access is stated on the venue page; details of step-free routes between garden, orangery and ballroom are not stated on the venue page [1]
- Accessible toilet: not stated on the venue page [1]
- Parking: on-site parking available [1]
- Public transport: nearest station Meulan - Hardricourt [1]
- Opening hours on the event day: access hours not stated on the venue page [1]
- Noise and curfew limits: not stated on the venue page [1]
- Weather fallback: orangery (glass structure) and ballroom indoors; capacity of each room not stated on the venue page [1]
- Mobility support: place seniors and reduced-mobility guests near the ceremony seating and the dining room entrance; ask the venue to confirm in writing the step-free route between garden, orangery and ballroom and the accessible toilet
- Alternatives: Lésigny has wheelchair access, parking, RER E and A access and allows external caterers, but starts at 7050 EUR [2]; Janvry has disabled access, parking for 160 vehicles and station Orsay Ville, 6300-7300 EUR [3]; Breteuil (1800 EUR) has adapted toilets in the gardens and dedicated garden paths but no stated step-free access to the Orangery [4][6]
- Public holidays: none within ±3 days [5]
- Holiday impact: no holiday is on or next to 2027-09-18 (a Saturday), so no surcharge or earlier booking is expected from holidays; peak wedding season demand still calls for early booking.
