## Summary
Full program for the relaxed wedding of 150 guests at Château d'Hardricourt on 2027-09-18, 18:00-01:00: garden ceremony with an orangery or ballroom fallback, cocktail, seated dinner, first dance, and a DJ-led dance floor until 01:00. The venue already includes a professional sound and lighting system and dedicated staff [2], so no sound, lighting or technician is hired separately. One vendor covers DJ and MC: Soirées dansantes (Paris, no travel fee within 150 km) at 600 EUR, the lowest published price of a 600-1350 EUR range [1]. That package also bundles its own sound and lighting, which duplicates the venue inclusion; no DJ-only price is published, so the full 600 EUR is budgeted and a DJ-only quote should be requested. The entertainment share is 500 EUR (10% of 5000 EUR); the cheapest compliant option costs 600 EUR, which exceeds the share by 100 EUR, and the published upper price of 1350 EUR would exceed it by 850 EUR, so the price must be confirmed by quote. Amplified music limits and the curfew are not stated on the venue page and must be confirmed with the venue; the 01:00 finish is planned in any case. Fireworks are permitted by the venue but not planned or priced.

Relaxed tone, no children. Seniors and reduced-mobility guests are seated near the ceremony seating and the dining room entrance, and the venue is asked to confirm in writing the step-free route between garden, orangery and ballroom before booking [2].

| Time | Block | Duration | Details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 18:00 | Welcome and guest arrival | 30 min | Soft background playlist by the DJ through the venue sound system; guests seated in the garden, seats reserved for seniors |

| 19:00 | Cocktail and photos | 60 min | Light lounge playlist in the garden; sunset near 19:57 gives good light for photos |
| 20:00 | Seated dinner | 120 min | Dinner indoors; MC introduces the couple, short speeches and toasts, low-volume background music |
| 22:00 | First dance and cake | 30 min | Couple's first dance, parents' dance, cake cutting; the venue lighting system sets the mood |
| 22:30 | Dance party | 120 min | DJ set: pop, disco, funk and house, with requests from guests; a seated chill zone stays near the dance floor for seniors |
| 00:30 | Last dances and farewell | 30 min | Final song chosen by the couple, guests leave; music ends at 01:00 |

Optional fireworks are permitted by the venue [2] but are not priced or planned in this budget.
