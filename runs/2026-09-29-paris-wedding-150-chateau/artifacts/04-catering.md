## Catering option
- Option: external approved caterer, mandatory at this venue. Château d'Hardricourt: external caterers not permitted, only approved vendors (Cirette, Lenôtre, Kaspia Réceptions, Réception Prestige) [1].
- Caterer: Cirette Traiteur (Normandy, Rouen, also events in the Paris region), 80-200 EUR per guest, 20-1500 guests; menus as cocktails, seated dinners or buffets [2].
- Format: seated dinner served at the table (orangery or ballroom). Reason: the full seated dinner is a [MUST] requirement (R-04), and table service suits seniors and reduced-mobility guests, who do not queue at a buffet. A buffet would be cheaper but does not meet R-04.
- Services offered by the caterer according to its listing, quoted as options and not assumed to be part of the 80 EUR price: professional service and setup, wedding cake and desserts, beverages (consumption-based or per-person packages), tableware, linens and furniture rental [2]. The venue already provides linen, staff and non-consumables [1], so tableware and linen can be dropped from the quote if the venue covers them.
- Timing: guests arrive at 18:00, garden ceremony, dinner served roughly 20:00-22:30, cake at the end, coffee until 01:00. Venue curfew is not stated on the venue page and must be confirmed [1].

- Calculation: 80 EUR × 150 guests = 12000 EUR; VAT treatment is not stated.
- Catering share: 1750 EUR (35% of 5000 EUR). Excess over the share: 10250 EUR.
